A document is judged on reading comfort, not decoration. Almost all of that is the text column.

## The measure

**60–75 characters per line.** This is the single rule that separates a typeset page from a text
dump. At 1240px wide, that means a column of roughly 620–700px — so an A4 artboard has generous
side margins, or two columns, never one edge-to-edge block.

- Body 15–17px at `line-height: 1.55–1.65`.
- Paragraph separation by space *or* indent, never both. First paragraph after a heading is not
  indented.
- Ragged right, not justified — justified text without hyphenation opens rivers of white.

## Scale and rhythm

- Heading scale steps clearly: H1 34–40, H2 24–26, H3 18–19, body 16. If two levels look similar,
  the hierarchy has failed.
- Space above a heading is larger than space below it, so it binds to the text it introduces.
- A baseline rhythm: every vertical space a multiple of the line height (e.g. 26px). This is why a
  well-set page feels calm.
- One family for body, at most one contrasting family for headings. Serif body with sans headings
  is the classic pairing; sans throughout is also fine. Never three.

## Page furniture

Every page carries a running head or footer and a page number. Margins are consistent across pages —
that repetition is what makes a set of artboards read as one document. Figures get numbered captions
below them ("Figure 3 — Latency under load"), tables get captions above.

## For a research paper

- Title block: title, authors, affiliations, then the abstract as a single indented block.
- Two columns, roughly 580px each with a 40px gutter, at 1240px wide.
- Numbered sections (1, 1.1, 1.2). Figures may span both columns.
- Body 14–15px in two columns — narrower measure means smaller type is still comfortable.
- A references list in a consistent style, and in-text citations that match it.

## Colour

Documents are near-monochrome. Black or near-black text on white or off-white, one accent used only
for links, rules and figure keys. A colourful document reads as a brochure.

## Before you call it done

Measure it: is the line length in range, or is the column running the full page width? Do the
heading levels visibly differ? Is the leading comfortable? Does each page have its furniture, with
the same margins? Is anything clipped at the bottom of a page, or is the last page half empty
because content was not flowed sensibly across artboards?
